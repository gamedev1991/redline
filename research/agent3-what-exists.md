# What Exists: Contract-Review / Clause-Analysis Products (Market Scan)

Research conducted 2026-08-28 via web search. 8 searches used, 0 pages fetched (web search snippets only). Every finding below carries a source URL; any field without solid evidence is marked "not found."

---

## 1. Terms of Service; Didn't Read (ToS;DR)

- **What it does**: Community-curated project that grades websites' Terms of Service and Privacy Policies A (best) to E (worst). Volunteer curators tag individual clauses as "good," "neutral," "bad," or "blocker." Offers a browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox, Opera) that shows a grade + short summary for supported sites.
- **Who it targets**: Consumers, privacy-conscious general public.
- **Pricing**: Free (nonprofit/volunteer project).
- **Most common complaint**: Not found — search did not surface specific user complaints or reviews from 2025/2026; coverage is limited to major/popular sites since it depends on volunteer review, which is a structural limitation rather than a sourced complaint.
- **Sources**: https://tosdr.org/en , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terms_of_Service;_Didn't_Read

## 2. DoNotPay ("AI lawyer" consumer legal assistant)

- **What it does**: Marketed itself as an "AI lawyer" that could generate legal documents, fight subscriptions/bills, and give legal advice/document review without a human lawyer.
- **Who it targets**: Consumers (individual, self-service legal tasks).
- **Pricing**: Not found in this search (subscription-based; historical FTC materials reference the 2021-2023 subscription period but no current price point was surfaced).
- **Most common complaint / risk**: The FTC found DoNotPay's claims that its AI could perform at the level of a human lawyer were deceptive, unsubstantiated, and untested — the company never hired or retained attorneys to verify the accuracy of its legal document generation or advice. The FTC finalized an order in Feb 2025 imposing a $193,000 fine, ongoing advertising restrictions, and a requirement to notify past subscribers.
- **Sources**: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/02/ftc-finalizes-order-donotpay-prohibits-deceptive-ai-lawyer-claims-imposes-monetary-relief-requires , https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/donotpay

## 3. Spellbook (AI contract drafting/review, Word add-in)

- **What it does**: AI contract drafting and review tool that integrates into Microsoft Word; flags risks, suggests redlines, drafts clauses in-document.
- **Who it targets**: Law firms and in-house legal teams (SMB to enterprise legal departments).
- **Pricing**: Not publicly listed / custom quotes. Reported estimates vary by source: roughly $20–$40/user/month for entry tier up to ~$179/user/month mid-tier in one estimate; another source cites Starter $99/user/month, Professional $149/user/month, Enterprise $199/user/month (10-seat minimum). Free trial (7 days) or demo-call gated.
- **Most common complaint**: Reviewers note the AI "sometimes glitches and is prone to making mistakes," requiring vigilant human review; some critics call it overbuilt, opaquely priced, and too narrow in scope relative to cost.
- **Sources**: https://www.g2.com/products/spellbook/reviews , https://www.hyperstart.com/blog/spellbook-pricing/ , https://www.aivortex.io/legal/compare/spellbook-pricing-2026/

## 4. LawDepot (legal document/contract template generator)

- **What it does**: Online legal-document builder — generates contracts, leases, and other templates via guided Q&A forms.
- **Who it targets**: Consumers and small businesses needing standard legal documents (not AI-native clause analysis — template-driven).
- **Pricing**: Roughly $7.50–$120.00 per document depending on type/access window; a "One-Year Pro" subscription runs $107.88–$155.88/year (~$8.99–$12.99/month) for unlimited access to one document category. 7-day free trial offered.
- **Most common complaint**: Billing/transparency issues — Trustpilot reviewers report unexpected charges, being billed for subscriptions they didn't intend to purchase, "free" documents carrying hidden fees, and auto-billing without clear notice.
- **Sources**: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/lawdepot.com , https://g2.com/products/lawdepot/reviews

## 5. LegalOn Technologies (AI contract review for legal teams)

- **What it does**: AI contract review platform pairing AI with attorney-authored playbooks/guidance to flag nuanced risks and insert one-click redlines.
- **Who it targets**: Legal teams — claims 3,800+ legal teams as customers (in-house counsel, law firms; skews enterprise/mid-market).
- **Pricing**: Enterprise pricing requires direct sales engagement (per-seat subscription). An "Individual" plan is cited at $550/month for unlimited reviews and full playbook/AI-assistant access.
- **Most common complaint**: Significant upfront time investment required to build/customize a "Playbook" before the tool is useful; AI occasionally produces false positives requiring human oversight; some customers cite high cost; tool cannot yet reference multiple documents together when a contract spans several files.
- **Sources**: https://www.g2.com/products/legalon/reviews , https://spellbook.com/learn/legalon-pricing , https://directory.lawnext.com/products/legalon-technologies/

## 6. Rocket Lawyer (online legal services + document platform)

- **What it does**: Legal document creation, e-signature, and "Ask a Lawyer" access to real attorneys, plus business formation services.
- **Who it targets**: Consumers, freelancers, and small businesses.
- **Pricing**: Subscription-based (monthly membership, e.g. one reported plan at $39.99/month); exact current tiers not fully captured in this search.
- **Most common complaint**: Billing practices and cancellation difficulty are the dominant complaint theme — recurring unauthorized monthly billing after free trials end, difficulty cancelling subscriptions, slow/unresolved refund requests, and double charges. (Trustpilot ratings themselves are high, 4.5–4.7/5, but BBB/ConsumerAffairs/PissedConsumer threads concentrate on billing.)
- **Sources**: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/rocketlawyer.com , https://www.consumeraffairs.com/legal/rocket-lawyer.html , https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/san-francisco/profile/legal-forms/rocket-lawyer-1116-143904/complaints

## 7. Genie AI (AI contract drafting/review assistant)

- **What it does**: AI "contract assistant" for drafting, reviewing, and negotiating contracts, including a risk-review feature for identifying loopholes.
- **Who it targets**: Small businesses (reportedly 92% of reviewers are small-business users) and individuals.
- **Pricing**: Not fully specified in search results; users describe free trials as "severely limited" and the paid tier as "a little costly for startups to afford."
- **Most common complaint**: Affordability for startups/small businesses and restrictive free-trial limits.
- **Sources**: https://www.capterra.com/p/10003121/Genie-AI/ , https://www.g2.com/products/genieai/reviews

## 8. Browser-extension ToS/T&C summarizers (category — e.g. "T&C Reader", "Terms & Policies Summarizer")

- **What it does**: Lightweight Chrome extensions that use AI (several explicitly built on Google Gemini) to summarize a webpage's Terms & Conditions or Privacy Policy into bullet points on demand; some (e.g. "T&See") add chat-style Q&A over the summarized text.
- **Who it targets**: General consumers browsing any site with a ToS/privacy policy.
- **Pricing**: Free (Chrome Web Store listings found had no paid tier surfaced in this search).
- **Most common complaint**: Not found as explicit complaint text; the closest sourced signal is inconsistent Chrome Web Store star ratings across similar extensions — e.g. "T&C Reader" averages 3.5/5 and "Terms & Policies Summarizer" averages 3/5, versus other similarly-named extensions ("Terms Summarizer," "AI Terms & Conditions Summarizer") showing 4.8/5 — suggesting quality/reliability is inconsistent across this crowded, low-differentiation extension category, but no specific complaint quotes were retrieved.
- **Sources**: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tc-reader/gimeoplgjodcifhdhgdpbejgdffknlnl , https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/terms-policies-summarizer/picabpijbpbfacfjmbjiepnfippeaded , https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tsee-ai-terms-conditions/glejkdljgdjljcokafadgnfhhgbcifoe
